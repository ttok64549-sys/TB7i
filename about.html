About the Studio

We don't just make games. We create worlds you'll fight to survive in.

// Our Story

Born in the Dark

The Black Studio was founded with a single obsession: to build horror experiences that feel real, grounded and unmistakably ours. Growing up surrounded by the dense streets and chaotic energy of Pakistani cities, we saw a setting the games industry had never truly explored.

Instead of imitating Western apocalypses, we asked a different question — what would the end of the world look like here? That question became Sector Zero.

Today we're a small, focused team building our flagship title in Unity 3D — with a commitment to authenticity, atmosphere and tension above all else.

◎

Our Mission

To craft original, story-driven horror games that represent South Asian settings with authenticity — and to prove that world-class games can be built from Pakistan.

◈

Our Vision

To become a recognised independent studio known for atmospheric, uncompromising survival horror — and to inspire the next generation of Pakistani developers.
