Sector Zero

A city has fallen. The infected own the streets. You are all that's left.

// Overview

Survive the Collapse

Sector Zero is an original 3D zombie-apocalypse survival game currently being developed by The Black Studio, led by CEO Muhammad Atiqur Rehman.

Set in a devastated version of Rawalpindi, Pakistan — with environments inspired by the streets and atmosphere of Rajabazar — Sector Zero places the player in a city overwhelmed by a deadly outbreak. Civilization has collapsed, the streets are abandoned, and dangerous infected creatures now control the city.

Players take the role of a survivor who must explore the ruined environment, search for essential resources, confront hostile zombies, and fight to stay alive.

Zombie Apocalypse Survival Action Horror Unity 3D
